Look at your display, not at a spec sheet.

Six test patterns for a Mac monitor: grayscale, colour bars, near-black, near-white, a 1-pixel grid and a uniform field. Use full screen and the display's native resolution.

Grayscale ramp: every step should be visible on a calibrated display.
Try LumePane's calibrationColour guideScroll test

What this can and cannot do. The browser can show reference steps, but it cannot read or change the panel's gamma, colour temperature or DDC/CI registers. A pattern that looks good on screen is not a calibration report. If banding, a colour cast or clipped blacks are visible here, the next step is a measured calibration in LumePane (gamma-table anchor + DDC/CI where available).
