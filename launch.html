Launch · evaluation program · no fake reviews

90 days of LumePane, for the first people who actually try it.

A 90-day evaluation is available for people who will run LumePane on a real Mac and report concrete issues. No purchase or favourable review is required; the limits are listed on this page.

How to claim one

  1. Open the support window at the bottom-right and choose “Order / code”.
  2. Include your macOS version and the Mac model.
  3. A private code is returned in the same thread; one per person.

Codes expire 90 days after issue. They are personal, not for resale, and not for public reposting. No purchase or review is required.

What you’re trying

LumePane is a macOS 14+ utility: a draggable orb that unfolds into a dock for screenshots, recording, lock/sleep, volume, brightness, window layouts and presets; DDC/CI display control with a gamma fallback; and per-frame mouse-wheel smoothing with per-app exclusions and a ⌘ bypass.

Honest limits: macOS exposes no public Apple Watch battery API; some third-party mice do not expose battery; trackpad gestures stay native. Smoothing is reshaping input, not creating it.

For creators and communities

For a community, newsletter or channel batch, use the support window, choose “Order / code”, and include the publishing location and requested quantity. Codes are private and can be disclosed as developer-provided.

No positive coverage is required. If the app is not right for your audience, saying so is more useful than a fake recommendation.
